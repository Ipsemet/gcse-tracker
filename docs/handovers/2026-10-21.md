## Organiser errors corrected (Harry should know)

- Shi'a imams: the sheet says no family link to Muhammad. They were his descendants via Ali and Fatimah.
- Cross of Nails is Coventry Cathedral, not Canterbury.
- Crusades: 11th–13th centuries, not 14th.
- "The LOVE of money is a root of all kinds of evil" (1 Timothy 6:10), not "money is the root".
- "Fighting has been ordained for you" is Qur'an 2:216, not 49:9.
- Greater jihad is the inner struggle, not holy war.
- The sheet's sacred-writings question is worth 6; AQA gives it 5. The bank uses 5.
